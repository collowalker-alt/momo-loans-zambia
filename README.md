## Important
This is a frontend prototype. The application interest form does not send or store data and is not connected to a lender. Calculator output is principal-only and excludes interest/fees. Confirm the legal business identity, licensing, loan products, rates, fees, privacy terms, contact details and lender-approved disclosures before publishing or accepting applications.
